Unreferenced Opcodes 476
What are unreferenced opcodes?

These are opcodes present in the energy model but not part of the 58 reference-group comparisons above. Most are LLVM-internal variants — different machine encoding forms of the same base instruction. For example, ADDWri, ADDXrs, ADDv16i8, ADDv8i8 are all variants of the ADD instruction. Each still has a properly assigned energy value and contributes to all estimates — they are simply not validated against published measurements because papers only publish values for canonical instruction forms.
